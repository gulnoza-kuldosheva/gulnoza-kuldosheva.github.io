Gulnoza Kuldosheva

PhD researcher in housing wealth inequality
LISER and University of Luxembourg

I am a PhD researcher at the Luxembourg Institute of Socio-Economic Research (LISER) and the University of Luxembourg.

I study how property wealth is distributed, how unequal it is, and how this changes over time. I look at the structural factors that shape access to housing wealth for different groups of the population, across time and space. Housing is the main part of most households' wealth and an important part of wellbeing.

My work uses linked administrative registers for Luxembourg: social security records merged with the national property register. The data cover the full resident population and include household structure, parent and child links, and labour market position.

My broader research interests are income and wealth inequality, and what inequality means for the welfare of different groups, by country of birth, gender and age.
